# EFMW external protocol — Greek-native reconstruction

**Research design, not a claim of physical validation.** The proposed EFMW layer organizes research, while the native mathematical civilization builds its own constructs from historically documented mathematical categories.

## Two deliberately isolated languages

**Native object language:** ὄν, μονάς, πλῆθος, μέγεθος, λόγος, ἀναλογία, ἁρμονία, κίνησις, ἀπόδειξις. Legitimate utterances concern existing magnitudes, distinctions, comparison, construction, and proof. No native value denotes nonexistence, and numeric zero is not a primitive.

**External audit metalanguage:** Lean 4 types, inductive constructors, standard natural numbers used in proofs of countable examples, signed timestamps/claim IDs, source metadata, JSON for portability, JavaScript for the UI. These tools do not imply a modern Greek ontology.

## Native investigation cycle

1. **Aisthesis:** record an appearance as an observation; do not infer a universal law.
2. **Diorismos:** state admissible objects, kinds and relations.
3. **Kataskeue:** perform a permitted construction.
4. **Apodeixis:** demonstrate a proposition under its declared premises.
5. **Aporia:** record contradictions and missing steps without inventing an answer.
6. **Mneme:** retain provenance, observations and full dependency graph.
7. **New logos:** generate a further admissible account only after prior records are preserved.

This is an *experimental assembled procedure*, not a direct quotation from any single Greek school.

## Formal work packages

**WP-A: Historicity.** Primary source edition + passage locator + translation + evidence type + divergence among schools. No anachronistic "Parmenides proved no zero."

**WP-B: Native grammar.** Unity is a different type from multitude; multitudes begin from dyad; magnitude expressions refer only to admitted givens and compositions; no constructor called nonexistence.

**WP-C: Proportions.** Abstract magnitude carrier; admissible repeated magnitudes; three-way comparison; relation checked with all positive multiples and order comparisons, following Euclid V.5 in abstract form. Prove reflexivity, symmetry and transitivity. This is an *axiomatization/definition*, not a reconstruction of Euclid's whole proof corpus.

**WP-D: Harmonics.** Positive integer string-length pairings for octave, fifth, fourth. Test exact cross-product relationships. This is an *external verification* of traditional ratios, not a proof of celestial sounds.

**WP-E: Cosmos model.** Source-labeled conceptual circles/recurrences. Never substitute the display for empirical orbital mechanics.

**WP-F: Independent encounter.** Show historical-model reasoning observations stripped of modern theoretical explanation. Preserve a frozen transcript before comparison.

## Zoo-style audit status schema

Use precisely **PASS, FAIL, UNRESOLVED, N/A** for each independent claim and each relevant test. N/A does not count as a positive result; a missing prerequisite should be UNRESOLVED.

Include explicit provenance classes: PRIMARY_SOURCE, SECONDARY_SCHOLARSHIP, MODERN_FORMALIZATION, COUNTERFACTUAL_EXTENSION, OBSERVATION. No claim is upgraded simply because the system has an elegant diagram.

A conclusion is **ESTABLISHED** only relative to the exact mathematical premises and verified implications; physical establishment requires observations and registered error bounds. Dependencies on UNRESOLVED premises propagate qualification, even when the derived internal proof compiles.

## CIRCUS circularity controls

- Every experimental constant has an origin, derivation or experimental fit label.
- The held-out observable is not accessible in premise selection or internal parameter tuning.
- All post-hoc index assignments are recorded.
- Every claim links to its source passages, dependencies and proof artifacts.

## MONSTER stress controls

- Change arbitrary diagram radii and drawing choices: mathematical results must not change.
- Perturb sounding-string lengths: distinguish robust consonance intervals from idealized ratios.
- Independently regenerate examples and formal Lean proofs.
- Swap historical traditions: inconsistent doctrines must not silently fuse.
- Test representative cases where a construction is inadmissible: report no constructed magnitude, not a "null being."

## Expected experiment outcomes

Success may be (a) a historically rigorous reconstruction, (b) an internally new and proven mathematical theorem, (c) a distinct empirically supported explanation, or (d) a reproducible negative result. **No outcome is promised in advance.**

## Launch criterion

The included Lean corpus and dashboard are a **starter platform**, not a "total" machine-checked Greek corpus. An institutional collaboration would need historians of ancient mathematics, classicists, formal-methods researchers, and domain physicists.
