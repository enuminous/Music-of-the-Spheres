# Ἁρμονία τοῦ Ὄντος — The Harmonic Cosmos

**A historically grounded, zero-excluding Greek-native mathematical civilization organized through an external EFMW research protocol.**

**Epistemic legend**

- **HISTORICAL:** attributed to an identified primary source or defensible historical account.
- **RECONSTRUCTION:** conceptual continuation assembled from Greek sources; not an ancient unified doctrine.
- **FORMAL MODEL:** a deliberately simplified mathematical construction amenable to external Lean 4 checks.
- **HYPOTHESIS:** a proposed explanation or physical claim requiring independent test.
- **UNRESOLVED:** a tension, missing derivation, or inaccessible source.

## I. Being prior to counting

The Eleatic challenge concerns what can be intelligibly said about Being and non-being. An experimental Parmenidean starting premise is: **nonexistence is not a mathematical state**. That is a *chosen interpretation and research constraint*, not a proof that physical disappearance or mathematical zero is impossible. We do not turn non-being into a special object, a vanishing magnitude, or an error token in the native language.

A valid attempt at construction has a defined admissible magnitude or relation. An inadmissible attempt is not a newly created member of the universe. The modern proof assistant may use metalinguistic judgments about type inhabitation and operation domains, but the native ontology does not.

Parmenides' strict Being creates a tension with plurality and motion. This reconstruction **preserves** rather than claims to solve it. The Eleatic strand studies the prerequisites of thought; astronomy, biology and mechanics study appearances. A proposed reconciliation through stable identity and changing appearances is **RECONSTRUCTION**, not an authenticated Eleatic theorem.

## II. The mathematical sevenfold

### Monas — μονάς

Unit or unity, the condition by virtue of which something is called one. Euclid VII.1 distinguishes unity from number. It need not be counted as an *arithmos* in that vocabulary.

### Plethos and arithmos — πλῆθος, ἀριθμός

Euclid VII.2 defines number as a multitude composed of units. Our native multitudes begin with a pair and extend by adjoining a further unit. A *singular* unity is not identical in type with a multitude. There is no native number-like object for non-being or zero.

This **does not imply** that all Greek mathematicians, across all eras, rejected every positional placeholder: late ancient astronomical calculation is a documented exception.

### Megethos — μέγεθος

Magnitude is investigated geometrically. A line, area or volume is not automatically an integer multitude. A line segment may be given, bisected, compared, or compounded with another segment under conditions of the same kind.

The diagonal and side of a square are admissible magnitudes even when no common magnitude measures both. This is fundamental to Greek mathematics rather than a failure to know decimal approximations.

### Logos — λόγος

Ratio is a relation between magnitudes of the same kind, not necessarily a written quotient. Euclid V.3–5 compares magnitudes through equimultiples: pairs of separately repeated magnitudes are greater, equal, or less together. Our Lean model represents this as equality of comparison outcomes across all admissible positive multitudes; this is a **minimal formal analogue**, not a complete formalization of Euclid Book V.

### Schēma — σχῆμα

Geometrical construction produces intelligible figures. Proof statements must identify their givens, legitimate constructions, and necessary consequences. An impossible construction is marked inadmissible, not assigned a special empty magnitude.

### Harmonia — ἁρμονία

Fitting together, particularly perceptible in musical consonance. Under controlled string conditions, shorter strings yield higher pitch. Traditional ratios between lengths include the double, sesquialter (three-to-two) and sesquitertian (four-to-three), corresponding to familiar consonant intervals.

Harmony is **not automatically** a force, a universal conservation law, or evidence of celestial sound.

### Apodeixis — ἀπόδειξις

Demonstration connects definitions and admitted statements. A statement without a valid demonstration is not upgraded to knowledge merely because it accords with a preferred philosophical system. This is the methodological antidote to a theory prematurely announcing itself as established.

## III. Multiple Greek schools, not a merged mythology

**Eleatics:** Parmenides, Zeno, Melissus. The being/change paradox remains open.  
**Early Pythagorean voices:** Philolaus and Archytas. Explore concordance of limit and unlimited and mathematical music; preserve uncertainty about late traditions attributed to Pythagoras personally.  
**Plato:** mathematical intelligibility, dialectic, *Timaeus* cosmic proportional construction. Do not equate Plato's narrative with measured cosmology.  
**Aristotle:** categories, causes, scientific demonstration, change, substance. Aristotle critiques the literal audible music of celestial bodies in *On the Heavens*.  
**Eudoxus, Euclid, Theaetetus:** equal ratios of incommensurable magnitudes, proof, classification.  
**Archimedes, Apollonius:** exhaustion, centers of weight, conics, floating bodies and geometrical analysis.  
**Aristoxenus:** music investigated through hearing, function, and melodic motion. Distinguish from pure mathematical tuning.  
**Astronomers:** Aristarchus, Eratosthenes, Hipparchus, Menelaus, Ptolemy, and others; competing celestial models, observed periods, chords, spherical geometry.  
**Practical mathematicians:** Heron, surveyors and mechanicians, optical writers.  
**Late-antique thinkers:** Diophantus, Pappus, Theon, Hypatia, Proclus, Eutocius and successive commentary traditions.

A cognitive envelope must be able to disagree with itself *by school and historical period* without silently rewriting one school as another.

## IV. The harmonic demonstration

**Given:** two comparable sounding strings, similarly tensioned and constructed. One has twice the sounding length of the other.

**Observed:** the shorter string ordinarily sounds an octave higher.

**Further constructions:** three-to-two length ratio produces a fifth, four-to-three a fourth. These are idealized comparisons; material stiffness, boundary conditions and listener perception all matter.

**Proposed reasoning:** if order is mathematically knowable in sound, investigate whether relationships of proportion also organize other observable domains.

**Boundary:** the step from musical consonance to a universal harmonic cosmos is a *hypothesis*. Planets do not thereby generate literal tones or obey string tuning laws.

## V. Astronomical appearances

A motion of a wandering star may be described through positions and periods, arcs and circles, intersections and recurrences. Different model families may explain the same appearances. Historic astronomy explored multiple competing models, including the exceptional heliocentric proposal of Aristarchus and elaborate geocentric arrangements. Their existence disproves the simplistic claim of one uniform ancient cosmology.

**Modeling rule:** the interactive spheres on the site are conceptual diagrams of periodic and relational ordering. Radius, animation speed and orbit placement are *display settings*, not measured astronomical parameters. No claims of accurate epicycles, gravitational orbits, precession or planetary music are made.

## VI. Mechanics, optics and living phenomena

- **Mechanics:** equality of moments on a balance, lever magnitudes and constrained equilibrium; the relation between ideal construction and material apparatus.
- **Buoyancy:** Archimedean investigation of floating bodies and displacement, within historical limits.
- **Optics:** geometry of sight and reflection. Ancient explanations of seeing varied and may be physically incorrect while retaining legitimate geometrical insights.
- **Living nature:** form, growth, proportion, variation, sensory reports and causal accounts. The tradition contains divergent physiological views.
- **Mind:** perception, memory, computation, insight, skill, demonstration and puzzles, arranged into an *experimental cognitive process*.

The project does **not** claim a historical proof of neural physics, consciousness, or cognition from harmonic musical ratios.

## VII. Native investigation cycle

1. **Aisthēsis:** encounter and record appearance.
2. **Diorismos:** identify objects, magnitude kinds and permitted relations.
3. **Kataskeuē:** construct a diagram, ratio, or ordered account.
4. **Apodeixis:** prove what follows from prior admitted statements.
5. **Aporia:** preserve the unresolved difficulty instead of filling it with a convenient value.
6. **Mnēmē:** remember premises, counterexamples, provenance and disagreements.
7. **Ananeōsis:** begin a revised account without erasing the earlier record.

These stages are **modernly assembled from historical vocabulary**. They are not a discovered single ancient algorithm.

### Native case: attempting to remove the whole from itself

The requested act yields no admissible positive remainder under the experiment's stipulated operations. There is no special null object. The native derivation simply ends as *inadmissible*. External formal verification can record the event as an unsuccessful construction. No statement about the existence of an actual cosmos follows from that convention alone.

### Native case: a square's diagonal

The diagonal and side are both admissible magnitudes. Their comparison need not yield a common measuring segment. A geometric proof may establish an incommensurability without introducing a modern decimal representation.

### Native case: changing magnitude

An Archimedean tradition could generate constructions bounding a curved region ever more tightly. The investigation may discover methods resembling certain features of contemporary analysis. **It must not be told in advance that the only successful destination is calculus.**

## VIII. The worldview as a complete cognitive research environment

A full model must organize:

- **Ontology:** what kinds of mathematical things may be spoken of.
- **Epistemology:** what counts as testimony, demonstration and observation.
- **Mathematics:** unity, number, magnitude, proportion, figure, proof.
- **Music:** sound, consonance, competing pitch theories, perception.
- **Cosmology:** cyclic appearances, competing diagrams, geometric ordering.
- **Nature:** mechanical, optical, meteorological and biological evidence.
- **Cognition:** perceiving, remembering, comparing, doubting, proving, revising.
- **Historiography:** direct sources versus reconstructions and lost works.
- **Audit:** independent logical checks and experimental comparison.

This is a **design of a research environment**, not a faithful record of one ancient system having completed all those fields.

## IX. EFMW and the Zoo: external obligations

The EFMW layer tracks hypotheses and their dependencies. The formal checker verifies precise math under stated premises. A Zoo-style audit attempts to break circularity or overreach:

- Did the model introduce a quantity merely by renaming an unmeasured assumption?
- Did it change its source tradition mid-proof?
- Is a successful mathematical identity being confused with a physical observation?
- Are historical statements being drawn from genuine primary passages?
- Does the physical prediction survive variation of arbitrary display choices?
- Can an independent reader recreate the same result?

Claims have **PASS / FAIL / UNRESOLVED / N/A** statuses. N/A cannot increase evidential support. A claim whose premise is unresolved cannot be called established merely because the consequence is formal.

## X. Counterfactual horizon

What if Greek mathematics had extended its methods of proportion, construction and exhaustion for many more generations while sustaining disagreements between philosophical schools?

Possible results include new geometrical treatments of motion, different methods for accumulation, unfamiliar invariants of harmonic figures, new astronomical models or carefully characterized limits to their techniques.

The experiment must accept equivalent reformulations, genuinely distinct discoveries, and negative results. **Neither novelty nor empirical truth is guaranteed.**

## XI. The whole as a carefully labeled poetic model

> What is intelligible may be distinguished. What is distinct may be compared. What is comparable may enter a relation. Some relations fit together in harmonious proportion. Motion and appearance provoke inquiry. Every constructed explanation remains answerable to demonstration and observation. No missing answer is promoted to an object of non-being.

The guiding name, **Ἁρμονία τοῦ Ὄντος**, is a proposed project title—*Harmony of Being*—not an ancient name for EFMW.

## XII. What remains unresolved

- Philosophically reconcile Eleatic changeless Being with the observable world without changing the premise.
- Develop a mathematically rich native continuity theory from allowable constructions, not modern definitions in disguise.
- Verify many more genuine historical theorems in a proof assistant using transparent foundational encodings.
- Represent the disputes among Aristoxenian and Pythagorean harmony without flattening them.
- Construct a prospectively frozen observation task and test independent physical explanation.
- Obtain historical/philological review from specialists on source editions and translation.
